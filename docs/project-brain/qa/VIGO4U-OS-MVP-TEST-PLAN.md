# VIGO4U OS MVP Test Plan

## Sprint 3 Finance
- Create invoice with three vehicles.
- Add one payment and allocate to two vehicles.
- Verify item balances.
- Verify invoice balance.
- Verify customer statement.
- Verify overpayment behavior.
- Verify staff cannot fetch financial fields.
- Verify customer cannot fetch another customer's data.

## Sprint 4 Workshop
- Staff opens assigned work order.
- Staff submits cost with receipt URL.
- Pending cost does not update vehicle cost.
- Admin approves cost.
- Approved cost updates vehicle cost once.
- Rejected cost remains audit-only.

## Sprint 5 Customer Portal
- Customer opens own dashboard.
- Customer sees own invoice and balance.
- Customer-visible document appears.
- Internal document is hidden.

## Sprint 6 Dashboard
- KPI totals match source data.
- Alert list links to detail pages.
- Staff role hides financial KPIs.

## Sprint 7 Launch
- Full mobile flow works.
- Error messages are user friendly.
- Audit log records write actions.
- Backup/export workflow exists.
